# OreoTunes

### Your music. Your mood. Your atmosphere. 🎧

**OreoTunes is a modern Android music player for people who want their local music library to feel as beautiful as a streaming app.**

Built with **Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Material 3**, OreoTunes combines powerful local music playback with immersive album artwork, dynamic colors, blurred album-art backgrounds, mood-based playlists, and a modern glass-inspired interface.

> 🎵 **Your music library, redesigned.**

---

## ✨ Why OreoTunes?

Most local Android music players focus primarily on playback controls.

OreoTunes focuses on the **entire listening experience**.

Your album artwork becomes part of the interface. Colors are extracted from the artwork, the background adapts to the currently playing song, and the Now Playing experience is designed to feel immersive rather than static.

---

## 🎵 Music Playback

OreoTunes is designed for local music playback with a modern Android interface.

- Local music library playback
- Albums, artists, songs, and playlists
- Folder-based music selection
- Crossfade between tracks
- Hi-Fi audio playback
- Bit-perfect playback mode where supported by the device and audio path
- Playback notification controls
- Media playback controls
- Playback statistics

---

## 🎨 Immersive Album-Art Experience

Album artwork isn't just displayed — it influences the entire listening atmosphere.

- Dynamic album-art backgrounds
- Blurred album-art atmosphere
- Album-art color extraction
- Dynamic interface colors
- Immersive Now Playing screen
- Modern glass-inspired UI
- Animated atmospheric background effects

The goal is simple:

**Every song should feel visually connected to its artwork.**
