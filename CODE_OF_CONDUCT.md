# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want OreoTunes to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive open-source project.

Everyone who participates in this project is expected to help create a positive environment for contributors, users, and maintainers.

## Expected Behavior

Please:

- Be respectful and considerate of others.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus on ideas and technical issues rather than individuals.
- Be patient with new contributors.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Respect different perspectives and experiences.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful comments.
- Deliberate intimidation or bullying.
- Sharing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Spam or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make the project community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer through the contact method provided in the repository.

Please do not publicly disclose sensitive information when reporting an issue.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or issues, or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a respectful and productive project environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related community spaces.
